<p>这些变量放到了一个特殊的section中了，看kernel的注释就明白什么意思了：</p>
<pre><code>
[== Undefined ==]
&quot;include/linux/init.h&quot;

/* Data marked not to be saved by software suspend */
#define __nosavedata __section(.data..nosave)

</code></pre>
